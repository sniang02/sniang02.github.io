I completed my PhD in the [Maasai project-team](https://team.inria.fr/maasai/) (Inria),
at the [Laboratoire J.A. Dieudonné](https://math.univ-cotedazur.fr/),
[Université Nice Côte d'Azur](https://univ-cotedazur.eu/), under the supervision of
[Prof. Charles Bouveyron](https://math.univ-cotedazur.fr/~cbouveyr/),
[Dr. Marco Corneli](https://math.univ-cotedazur.fr/~mcorneli/),
and [Prof. Pierre Latouche](https://lmbp.uca.fr/~latouche/).
I defended my thesis on **2nd October 2026**.

My PhD research focused on **deep generative models for the joint analysis of networks
and continuous data**. More broadly, my research interests lie at the intersection of
*probabilistic modelling*, *statistical learning*, and *machine learning*, with a particular
emphasis on graph-structured data, deep generative models (variational autoencoders,
latent variable models), probabilistic inference, and applied statistics.

I am now a postdoctoral researcher at [INRIA Rennes](https://www.inria.fr/fr/centre-inria-universite-rennes)
in the [LACODAM team](https://team.inria.fr/lacodam/).

Feel free to reach out by email for any question related to my research.
